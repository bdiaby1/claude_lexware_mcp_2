# Todd V — Todd's Lines (Struktur der Interaktion)

> **Wichtig (Todd's eigener Note):** *Das Seeding des Close zählt viel mehr als die finale Close-Line.*
> Lines sind **Trainingsräder** / Inspiration — Ziel ist, die **Struktur** zu verinnerlichen und eigene, kalibrierte Varianten zu bauen. Roboter-Skript verliert.

Die Interaktion läuft durch fünf Phasen: **Open → Premise → Evaluation → Narrative → Close.**

---

## 1. Open
Kontakt herstellen, Grund fürs Rübergehen.
- "I love your look, so I had to come over and find out if you're as cool as your look suggests."
- "You look like trouble in the most adorable way."
- "You look so [New York / Berlin / …]." (an ihre Ausstrahlung anpassen)
- "Hey, you looked bored over here, so I thought I'd come over and amuse… myself ;)"
- "Sorry, I saw you looking at me, so I had to come over and say hi."
- "Did you really just do that?!" (wenn sie was Teasebares macht)

## 2. Premise
Ihr etablieren, wer ihr zueinander seid — spielerisch, charged, leicht push/pull.
- "You and I are not going to get along."
- "I think you might be the girl my mother warned me about."
- "Part of me loves you and part of me hates you."
- "Do you know what I love about you… absolutely nothing ;)… just kidding etc."
- (2+ Mädels) "You seem [nett/positiv]… you, (pause)… you're [trouble/devious/…]."
- "Please stop being so cute, it's distracting."

## 3. Evaluation (Qualifying — sie soll sich beweisen)
Du bewertest sie. Dreht das Script: sie qualifiziert sich für **dich**.
- "Please tell me you're not one of those [type] people." → "Hmm… that's actually pretty cool. Now I'm intrigued; tell me more."
- "You're not so bad to talk to… are you also [Trait, den du magst]?"
- "What am I going to find out about you in about an hour that's going to totally convince me you're a bad idea?"
- "You're cute, but what have you got going for you besides your looks that will keep me intrigued?"
- "If you were me, would you date you?"

## 4. Narrative (gemeinsame Story + Comfort)
Zeitverzerrung, "wir kennen uns schon länger", persönliche Offenbarung.
- "Wow, I can't believe I just met you, it feels like so much longer…"
- "I'm really enjoying this, but I don't think we should make the sex tonight." (charged, spielerisch)
- "Where did you even come from? I wasn't planning on meeting anyone tonight."
- "Tell me something about you that would surprise me."
- "I don't normally tell this to someone I just met but [persönliche Story]."
- "Please don't fall in love with me… I'm bad news ;)"

## 5. Close (Seeding > Line)
- "It was nice talking to you, what's your #?"
- "Can I trust you? Okay, in that case we can hang out for a while longer I guess…"
- "You can come in, but I have to get up early, so it's just for a little bit." (bounce/pull-back)
- "I'm actually going the same way… I'll split a cab with you if you promise to behave."
- "Sounds good, what does your schedule look like this week?"
- "Hmm… so if we wanted to continue this, how would we do that?" (lässt SIE lösen)

---

## Wie wir Lines in der Praxis nutzen
- Nicht auswendig-abfeuern. **Phase erkennen → Intent der Phase treffen → in deinen Worten.**
- Push/Pull, Amused Mastery, Qualifying sind die *Mechaniken* hinter den Lines — die zählen, nicht der Wortlaut.
